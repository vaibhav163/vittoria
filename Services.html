Core Solutions

Nine verticals,
one accountable partner.

Every service below is delivered under the same quality management system and BCAS-cleared operating standard — so accountability never gets lost between vendors.

01

Aircraft Leasing

Dry & Wet Lease

Vittoria Aviation structures dry and wet lease arrangements that give airlines and charter operators fleet flexibility without long-term capital exposure. We manage the commercial, technical and regulatory workstreams that sit behind every lease — from aircraft sourcing to delivery condition and return compliance.

  • —Dry lease (ACMI-adjacent) structuring
  • —Wet lease with crew & maintenance
  • —Lease redelivery & condition audits
  • —Regulatory & DGCA coordination
02

MRO & Cabin Maintenance

Line & Turnaround

Our line maintenance and cabin readiness teams work to airline turnaround windows, keeping aircraft dispatch-ready between rotations. Cabin maintenance programs cover seat, galley and lavatory serviceability, cleanliness standards, and defect rectification logged against your maintenance system.

  • —Line maintenance & transit checks
  • —Cabin readiness & deep clean programs
  • —Defect logging & rectification tracking
  • —AOG support coordination
03

Airline Representation

Commercial & Regulatory Liaison

Vittoria Aviation acts as an airline's on-ground presence across Indian airports — handling commercial coordination, slot and permissions liaison, and day-to-day regulatory interface, so foreign and non-resident carriers get consistent local representation without standing up their own station.

  • —Commercial & sales representation
  • —Slot & permissions liaison
  • —Regulatory & airport authority interface
  • —Local operational point of contact
04

Ground Support Equipment

Leasing & Fleet Maintenance

We lease and maintain the tarmac equipment fleets that keep aircraft turnarounds on schedule — tow tractors, belt loaders, GPUs, air-start units and pushback tugs — held to a 98% serviceability target through a preventive maintenance program rather than reactive repair.

  • —GSE fleet leasing (short & long term)
  • —Preventive maintenance scheduling
  • —98% serviceability standard
  • —Spares & consumables management
05

Flight Clearance Agency

Permits & Non-Scheduled Handling

For non-scheduled, charter and overflight operations, we manage the permit and refuelling coordination chain end to end — landing and overflight permits, refuelling arrangements, and ground handling for aircraft that don't have a standing station presence in India.

  • —Landing & overflight permits
  • —Refuelling coordination
  • —Non-scheduled handling
  • —Slot & documentation support
06

Airport & Cargo Management

Business Operations

We take on end-to-end business management for airport and cargo operations — from warehouse and apron process design to staffing and vendor coordination — for operators who want operational control without building an in-house team from zero.

  • —Cargo warehouse operations
  • —Apron & ramp process management
  • —Vendor & staffing coordination
  • —Business performance reporting
07

Consulting & Quality Audits

Independent Advisory

Led by qualified aviation auditors, our consulting practice reviews ground operations, safety management and quality systems against regulatory and airline-specific standards, then works with your team to close the gaps — not just report on them.

  • —Ground operations quality audits
  • —Safety management system review
  • —Regulatory readiness assessments
  • —Corrective action follow-through
08

Aviation Training & Manpower

Certified Deployment

We train and deploy certified ground operations manpower — ramp agents, load controllers, security-cleared personnel — to airline and airport standards, and can staff a full shift roster or backfill specific roles on demand.

  • —Certified ground staff training
  • —Security-cleared manpower deployment
  • —Shift roster & backfill support
  • —Recurrent & compliance training

Robotic Dry Wash

Automated Exterior Cleaning

Our automated, waterless exterior cleaning technology removes contaminants that add drag and weight without the water consumption, runoff handling, or extended ground time of conventional washing — cutting turnaround time while supporting fuel-efficiency goals.

  • —Waterless, low-residue formulation
  • —Reduced turnaround ground time
  • —Lower water & runoff footprint
  • —Scheduled fleet-wide programs
