Vittoria Aviation is a BCAS-cleared Auxiliary Service Provider — regulated under the Bureau of Civil Aviation Security, Ministry of Civil Aviation, Government of India.

What BCAS Clearance Means

A security clearance, not just a business license.

The Bureau of Civil Aviation Security vets Auxiliary Service Providers operating within the security envelope of Indian airports — covering personnel background verification, access control discipline, and adherence to national civil aviation security programs. Clearance is a prerequisite for any vendor working airside or in security-restricted areas.

Every Vittoria Aviation service line — from GSE leasing to cargo operations — runs inside this cleared operating envelope, which is why airlines can engage us across multiple verticals under one security posture rather than re-vetting a new vendor for each function.
